## MANUAL STEPS FOR YOU

1. Install the latest preview APK on both phones and allow camera access. The
   **Scan QR** button starts the in-app camera scanner. It reads QR codes with
   the bundled ML Kit barcode model included in the APK; it does not open the
   Google Code Scanner UI or need Google Play services, internet, or a first-use
   model download. Tap **Scan another QR** to retry the same code.
2. Complete first-time setup and sync on both phones while online, and load
   vouchers onto the payer phone. After that, the payment exchange uses the two
   cameras and QR codes; no Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC, internet, or backend request
   is used during payment. Turn on airplane mode on both devices to test it.
   The receiver displays a request QR; the payer scans it, approves, and shows
   the signed proof QR frame(s); the receiver scans every frame and verifies
   and stores the proof on-device. The payer's spendable voucher balance drops
   immediately. The receiver's Home screen shows the received amount as a local
   pending credit once the proof is verified.
3. A received offline credit is visible on the receiver immediately but is not
   spendable offline again under the current voucher protocol. It becomes an
   online wallet credit only after the receiver reconnects and the backend
   redeems the proof. No backend or bank account can change while both devices
   are disconnected. Do not treat the local pending amount as a confirmed bank
   deposit.
4. Only OfflinePay request/proof QR codes are accepted by this offline flow. A
   regular bank UPI QR is not an OfflinePay transfer QR.
