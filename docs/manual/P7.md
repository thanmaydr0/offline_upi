## MANUAL STEPS FOR YOU

1. Install the new preview APK on both phones and allow camera access. The
   scanner fix uses Android's dedicated Google QR scanner when available, and
   keeps the in-app camera preview as a fallback. On Pay/Receive, tap **Scan QR**
   to open it; after each proof frame it reopens automatically until the flow
   finishes.
2. While online, open the scanner on both phones once before testing in airplane
   mode. Google Play services may need to download its scanner module on first
   use; after that, code detection runs on-device and does not need internet.
3. Complete setup/sync on both phones and load vouchers on the payer. Then put
   both phones in airplane mode. The receiver creates an OfflinePay request QR;
   the payer scans it, approves the payment, and displays proof QR frame(s) for
   the receiver to scan. Wait for **Payment received**. The payer's spendable
   offline balance decreases locally, and the receiver saves the incoming amount
   locally as pending settlement. Reconnect later to settle with the backend.
4. Only OfflinePay request/proof QR codes are accepted by this offline flow. A
   regular bank UPI QR is not an OfflinePay transfer QR.

   Physical-device validation remains unperformed from this workspace: `adb
   devices -l` returned an empty device list on 2026-10-19, so camera focus,
   brightness/glare, scan speed, and the two-phone airplane-mode flow could not
   be observed here. No hardware guarantee is claimed. Availability was checked
   from the project root with:

   ```powershell
   adb devices -l
   ```
