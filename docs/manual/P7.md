## MANUAL STEPS FOR YOU

1. Install the new preview APK on both Android phones. The scanner and Bluetooth
   socket module are native code, so older APKs and Expo Go cannot run this flow.
   Complete first-time setup and load vouchers on the payer while online.
2. In Android Bluetooth settings, pair the two phones once. Allow Camera and
   Nearby devices permissions. Then turn on airplane mode and switch Bluetooth
   back on on both phones. Wi-Fi and mobile data can stay off; no NFC is needed.
3. Receiver: open Receive, enter the exact amount, and tap Create my QR and receive.
   Keep Receive open. A new request is valid for five minutes.
4. Payer: open Pay. The bundled ZXing scanner opens automatically. Scan the
   receiver's new QR. Select their paired phone if multiple devices are listed,
   then tap Authenticate and pay and approve the biometric prompt. Previously
   chosen phones are remembered. The signed proof is sent by Bluetooth, and the
   receiver verifies and saves it locally before returning a signed receipt.
5. Check Payment received on the receiver and Payment delivered on the payer.
   Payer vouchers decrease locally; receiver Home shows Received offline as a
   pending credit. This protocol does not support re-spending incoming credits
   offline. Internet is needed later for backend redemption and payout, not for
   the two-phone exchange. A local receipt is not a bank-account balance update.
6. If delivery is interrupted, leave/open Receive on the receiver and tap Retry
   delivery on the payer. The saved proof is reused, not signed/spent again.
   Exact previously received proofs can be acknowledged after restart without
   crediting twice. Do not create a new amount until delivery is acknowledged.
7. After reconnecting, open Home/Sync to upload pending proofs for backend
   verification. A payout VPA is required for payout, not for recording and
   redeeming the received proof. Invalid/double-spent proofs can still fail
   backend settlement: offline signature checks cannot see another offline
   phone's conflicting spend.

Hardware validation is outstanding: adb devices -l lists no attached phones.
The JavaScript tests mock the native scanner/radio boundary; they do not prove
camera focus, Android pairing, radio performance, or OEM compatibility. The
cloud build must compile the native Kotlin module. No instant/zero-bug hardware
guarantee is claimed.
